This Privacy Policy explains how Yiorgos Georghiou, trading as Lock In ("we", "us", "Lock In") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the Lock In app (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Lock In is operated by Yiorgos Georghiou, trading as Lock In, a sole trader based in Cyprus. For any privacy question, contact us at yiorgosgeorghiou@yahoo.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Name and email address
  • Date of birth or age confirmation, to verify you meet the minimum age requirement
  • Subscription status and plan type (monthly or yearly), and trial status. This is provided to us by Apple; see Section 4

Health and activity data you choose to log

  • Workouts and training sessions
  • Meals, calories, and macronutrients, only if you turn calorie tracking on. It is off by default
  • Habit completion and repeat schedules
  • Water intake
  • Schedule entries (e.g. lectures, shifts, training times)
  • Journal entries. Any free-text reflections you write are stored so you can look back on them. We treat this content as private by default and do not read it for any purpose other than operating the Service, unless required by law or you report a problem that requires us to investigate.

Payment information

Lock In is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple, not Lock In, processes your payment and collects your billing details. We do not receive or store your card number, and we do not see your full payment details. Apple shares with us only what's needed to grant you access to the Service: your subscription status (active, expired, in trial, cancelled) and which plan you're on. See Apple's own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy for how Apple handles payment data.

Technical and usage data

  • Device type, operating system, app version
  • Crash reports and basic usage analytics (e.g. which screens are used)

3. Why we collect it (legal basis)

  • To provide the Service you subscribed to (performance of a contract)
  • To verify and maintain your subscription (performance of a contract)
  • With your consent, for health and fitness data specifically, since this is a special category of data under GDPR
  • To keep the Service secure and improve it (legitimate interest)
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records

4. How we use your data

  • To operate core features: training, fuel, habits, water, work, your journal, and your Discipline Index
  • To send service emails (subscription confirmations, password resets, important updates)
  • With your separate opt-in, to send motivational reminders or product news
  • To fix bugs and understand how the Service is used, in aggregate

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your journal entries or health data to serve you advertising.

5. Who we share it with

  • Apple: for subscription payment processing and to verify your subscription status
  • Supabase: to store your account and activity data securely
  • USDA FoodData Central: when you search for a food, your search term is sent to this public U.S. government nutrition database to return results. No other personal data is shared with them

We only share what each provider needs to do its job, under contracts that require them to protect your data. We do not share your journal entries or personal health data with any third party for marketing purposes.

6. International data transfers

If your data is processed outside the UK/EEA (for example, by a US-based hosting provider, or by Apple for payment processing), we rely on approved safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, and only work with providers that offer an adequate level of protection.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and journal data for as long as your account is active, including through any period your subscription has lapsed but your account still exists. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 90 days, except where we must keep records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes. Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription. See Section 4 of the Terms of Service for how to cancel.

8. Your rights

Under UK/EU GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email yiorgosgeorghiou@yahoo.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure hosting, to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

10. Children's privacy

Lock In is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. We will notify you of material changes by email or in-app notice before they take effect.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: yiorgosgeorghiou@yahoo.com.
